**Kyung Hee University Research Institute of Industry-University Cooperation Foundation** · 2022

This algorithm recovers 4D light-field information from a small number of images captured at different focal planes. It uses geometric transformations instead of 2D Fourier transforms, which shortens computation time. It also works with an arbitrary point-spread function, so data points can be localized below the diffraction limit.
