Professional Summary

Optical engineering researcher specializing in optics, photonics, and nanofabrication, with experience in super-resolution microscopy, interferometric scattering microscopy, light-field microscopy, spectroscopy, and 3D imaging. Currently developing high-speed volumetric microscopy for deep-tissue imaging in vivo.

Experience

  • 2024 - 2025

    Los Angeles, CA

    Research and Development Engineer
    University of California, Los Angeles, Intelligent Optics Laboratory
    Advisor: Dr. Liang Gao
    • Content-aware foveated camera for multi-target tracking. Developed the foveated camera, designed the dynamic optical architecture, optimized the target-tracking algorithms, and integrated the hardware and software interfaces.
    • NIR-II SLIM for in vivo deep-tissue 3D imaging. Developed the microscope, designed optical architectures including Dove prism arrays and cylindrical lenses in Zemax OpticStudio, optimized PSF calibration, and implemented Richardson–Lucy deconvolution in MATLAB and Python.
  • 2022 - 2024

    Ann Arbor, MI

    Graduate Researcher
    University of Michigan, Bioplasmonics Laboratory
    Advisor: Dr. Somin Eunice Lee
    • Phase intensity nanoscope (PINE). Developed a super-resolution method that operates without photobleaching by imaging gold nanorods at different polarization states and localizing them below the diffraction limit with the FISTA algorithm.
    • Soft multimaterial photonic devices for plasmonic super-resolution imaging. Built a compact integrated nanoscopic correction (mINC) device from liquid crystal and soft materials with a film polarizer and a film quarter-wave plate; the applied voltage tunes the phase retardation to acquire different polarization states.
    • Interferometric phase intensity nanoscopy (iPINE). Built an interferometric scattering (iSCAT) imaging system with a LabVIEW-controlled phase-intensity module (liquid crystal, quarter-wave plate, and linear polarizer), extracted nanorod phase from elliptical Airy patterns, and reconstructed images with FISTA.
  • 2022 - 2022

    Seoul, South Korea

    Researcher
    Kyung Hee University Research Institute of Industry-University Cooperation Foundation
    • Light-field extraction from multi-focus images. Developed an algorithm that extracts 4D light-field information from a few images taken at different focal points using geometric transformations; avoiding 2D Fourier transforms shortens computation, and the method handles arbitrary point-spread functions for sub-diffraction localization.
  • 2019 - 2020

    Seoul, South Korea

    Undergraduate Researcher
    Kyung Hee University, Quantum Information Display Laboratory
    Advisor: Dr. Jaewu Choi
    • Strain-dependent electrical and optical properties of single-layer polycrystalline graphene. Grew graphene by PECVD, transferred it to a self-developed strain device, designed the measurement station, and analyzed the optical changes under strain.
  • 2017 - 2018

    Seoul, South Korea

    Undergraduate Researcher
    Kyung Hee University, Diouf Laboratory
    Advisor: Dr. Baye Boucar Diouf
    • Kerr microscope for magnetic domains of powdered ferromagnetic materials. Measured the magneto-optical Kerr effect with a 638 nm laser under coil-controlled magnetization; prepared co-sputtered and spin-coated Fe powder films and characterized them by SEM.
